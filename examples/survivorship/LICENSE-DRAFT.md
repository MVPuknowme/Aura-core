# SKYGRID-protocol License of Survivorship

**Status: unsigned proposal for legal and commercial review. No license, inheritance right, agency, account access, or transfer is activated by this document, a checkbox, a repository commit, or a demo receipt.** An appropriately licensed attorney should adapt the rider to the parties, jurisdiction, existing estate documents and service contracts before execution.

### 2. Proposed grant
Upon execution by authorized parties, the licensor would grant the named licensee a nonexclusive, time-limited right to access only the SaaS functions identified in Schedule A for its agreed business purposes. No sublicense, redistribution, source-code delivery, exclusivity, trademark, patent or ownership grant is included unless expressly scheduled. Commercial hosting of owner-controlled implementation code requires a separate written grant.

The lease describes a software subscription, not a lease of real property, GPU capacity, a security, insurance, a bank product or a financial guarantee. Third-party compute and network costs require their own approved orders.

### 3. What survives
Subject to law and the executed agreement, the parties intend that death, incapacity or a change in authorized personnel alone will not automatically end an otherwise current, paid service license. A lawful representative may administer only the rights already held by the represented party, within verified authority and the agreed scope.

If the licensor dies or becomes incapacitated, the rights and obligations would be administered by a person or entity legally authorized to do so; this rider does not select an heir, transfer the licensor's copyrights, or compel a provider to continue an unfunded service. If the licensee requires a replacement operator, access changes follow section 4. An unresolved succession claim may suspend the requested access change without automatically terminating an otherwise unaffected customer license.

Confidentiality, accrued payment obligations, applicable ownership reservations, prior valid licenses and agreed dispute provisions survive termination as provided in the executed agreement. No perpetual hosting, guaranteed recovery or uninterrupted availability is promised by this draft.

### 4. Succession procedure
A proposed operator or representative submits a scoped request through a verified channel. Before access changes, the operator must validate the requester's identity, the affected account and assets, current authority, relevant event evidence, required disclosure consent, third-party limitations, conflicts and revocations. Death, incapacity and voluntary delegation require different evidence; an outage, unanswered message or missed heartbeat is not proof of death or incapacity.

The proposed operating policy requires two distinct authorized reviewers and a recorded scope, expiry and decision. This is a product design choice, not a statement that a two-person review alone satisfies law. A dispute, missing evidence, contradictory authority or revoked designation holds the requested change for review. Any legally required exception or court direction must be handled by qualified staff.

A verified representative uses a separate attributed account and must not impersonate Michael Vincent Patrick. An authenticated, lawful process must support revocation and correction. Access rights must be rechecked at every protected operation, including export and delegation.

### 5. Service continuity
A service outage may select a previously authorized backup or read-only route without activating succession. Location and policy guide the P0 route choice; unavailable authority or routes cause a hold. This prototype models an Oregon policy only. It does not establish an Oregon legal nexus, locate a device, authorize international data transfer, or switch a production network.

Proposed recovery, retention, support and export windows must be specified and funded in Schedule A. The prototype uses a 24-hour simulated authority window solely for demonstration; it is not a legal deadline. Backup testing, recovery objectives, staffing and provider acceptance remain implementation requirements.

### 6. Privacy and receipts
Disclosure is limited to data that the requesting party is authorized to receive. Personal communications, customer content, secrets, credentials, private keys and unrelated account records are excluded from the demo. Production document intake requires a secure, access-controlled channel; do not put estate or identity documents into a public repository.

A receipt should record scoped decision metadata and evidence references with minimal personal data. The demo exports unsigned JSON and an optional SHA-256 checksum. A checksum can assist byte comparison against a trusted copy; it does not prove identity, authenticity, time, legal authority, a payment or an external event. Production would need independently verified authority, managed signing keys, trusted timestamps and protected audit storage.

### 7. Rights retained and prior grants
Michael Vincent Patrick retains the rights he actually controls in scheduled owner-controlled material. Existing valid MIT, CC0 and other licenses remain effective for the material to which they apply. Dependencies and third-party contributions retain their own terms. This rider does not retroactively revoke grants, recover public-domain rights, relicense someone else's work or certify sole authorship of every repository file.

Reference the repository's current LICENSE and docs/licensing/exclusive-rights.md. File/version provenance must be reviewed before any commercial grant. No repository-wide or portfolio-wide ownership transfer is made.

### 8. Term, fees and termination
Fees, billing cycle, taxes, currency, support level and termination rules are **unpriced and unagreed**. No funds are charged or collected by this prototype. A signed order must state the subscription term, renewal mechanism, cancellation/refund rules, export window and handling of nonpayment. Suspension for security or lack of authority should be limited to the affected operation where feasible.

Risk allocation, warranties, liability limits and dispute provisions remain for the signed master agreement and legal review; mandatory rights remain unaffected. Source escrow is optional and requires a separate agreement, deposited materials, an escrow provider and defined release events. No escrow exists by virtue of this draft.
