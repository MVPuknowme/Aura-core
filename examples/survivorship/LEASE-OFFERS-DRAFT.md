## Acceptance criteria for an evaluation
1. An outage selects only the agreed simulated route and never establishes death or incapacity.
2. Missing evidence, a dispute, revocation or expiry holds the requested succession operation.
3. Sample successor access stays read-only and scoped.
4. Receipts identify simulation mode and do not represent verified legal authority.
5. Any proposed production release has separate authentication, tenant isolation, server-side policy enforcement and end-to-end tests.
