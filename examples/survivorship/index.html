Receipt boundaries
Exports include the inputs, decision, simulation label and an optional SHA-256 checksum. They contain no cryptographic signature or trusted timestamp.
A checksum is not proof of identity, legal authority, an external event or payment. These receipts use a new demo schema and have not been validated by Aura-Core PNPK.
