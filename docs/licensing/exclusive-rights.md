## Current licensing direction

The root [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) reserves proprietary rights in original owner-controlled material and new contributions. Commercial hosting, redistribution or integration requires a separate written license, subject to existing grants and applicable law. An exclusive grant to any partner must expressly identify the rights and material in an executed agreement; the repository notice itself assigns nothing.

No partnership is presumed. Software ownership, ownership of physical capacity, customer invoicing and third-party licenses are separate records. Where the founder owns both software and capacity, the 3.5% software/system share and 96.5% capacity share are two accounting components attributable to the same owner, not two receipts of the gross amount.
