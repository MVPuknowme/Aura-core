## Authorship and provenance rules for future proofs

For each major MVPuknowme proof of concept, preserve:

1. A dedicated source directory with a README describing the product and scope.
2. A dated feature branch rather than direct default-branch development.
3. Human-readable commit messages describing why each change exists.
4. Tests or reproducible validation commands where applicable.
5. A pull request authored from the `MVPuknowme` account.
6. Commit SHA and merge SHA in the evidence manifest.
7. Release or build artifacts with cryptographic hashes when practical.
8. A statement distinguishing implemented behavior from proposals or unverified claims.
9. A mark notice using `™`/`SM` unless and until registration permits use of `®`.
10. No claim that a GitHub timestamp alone proves every legal element of authorship, ownership, priority, or trademark rights.
11. Historical artifacts must never be backdated or rewritten to manufacture provenance; preserve originals and add later attestations separately.

## U.S. IP notice

The U.S. Copyright Office states that copyright protection attaches to qualifying original works of human authorship when they are fixed in a tangible form; registration can provide additional legal benefits and a public record.

The USPTO states that `TM` or `SM` may be used to indicate a claimed mark even without a federal registration. The federal registration symbol `®` should be used only for a mark that is actually federally registered and only in connection with the registered goods/services.
