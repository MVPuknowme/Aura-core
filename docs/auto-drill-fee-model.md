# Auto-Drill Aura-Core AI Operation TM — Fee Model

Operator: Michael Vincent Patrick / MVPuknowme
System family: SKYGRID / Aura-Core
Status: planning documentation for partner review

## Purpose

Auto-Drill Aura-Core AI Operation TM is the advisory preflight layer for Aura-Core routing and capacity planning.

It is designed to evaluate approved workload demand, available opt-in capacity, readiness, and fee treatment before a production workflow is activated.

## Revenue Flow

```text
Approved workload revenue
  -> participating node and partner allocation
  -> Aura-Core routing-layer retention
  -> creator allocation from Aura-Core retention
```

## Default Fee Terms

Aura-Core routing-layer retention:

```text
3.5% of approved routed workload revenue
```

Creator allocation for Michael Vincent Patrick / MVPuknowme:

```text
3% of Aura-Core's retained routing-layer fee
```

The creator allocation is calculated from Aura-Core's retained fee, not from total gross workload revenue, unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

## Example

```text
Gross approved workload revenue: $10,000.00
Aura-Core routing retention at 3.5%: $350.00
Creator allocation at 3% of $350.00: $10.50
```

## Guardrails

- Participation must be opt-in and agreement-based.
- This model does not authorize automatic use of outside devices or networks.
- This model does not move funds by itself.
- This model does not guarantee revenue.
- Production use requires partner approval, compliance review, and technical validation.
