# Commercial evidence ladder

Snapshot date: 2026-08-29

Repository activity is not customer traction. Use this ladder to prevent forecasts, internal tasks, and payment advances from being reported as software revenue.

| Stage | Minimum evidence | Financial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Offer defined | Scope, price, delivery window, exclusions, and named owner | Pipeline only |
| Qualified need | Customer record, dated need, decision maker, and next action | Pipeline only |
| Accepted order | Signed agreement, purchase order, or unambiguous written acceptance | Contracted amount; not collected revenue |
| Invoice issued | Invoice ID, amount, entity, terms, and delivery reference | Receivable; not collected cash |
| Payment settled | Processor/bank settlement ID and reconciliation | Cash received; recognition still follows accounting policy |
| Delivery accepted | Dated deliverable receipt and customer acceptance | Supports recognition and renewal evidence |
| Recurring revenue | Active contract, billing cadence, settlements, renewals, and churn records | Report MRR/ARR only from qualifying recurring contracts |

## Protocol Validation Sprint

The internal `$250 Protocol Validation Sprint` task is an offer/work item until customer evidence advances it through this ladder. A backlog issue, forecast, outreach draft, payment link, or unpaid invoice does not establish booked revenue.

The private commercial record should include:

- customer legal name and authorized contact;
- problem statement and success criteria;
- approved scope and exclusions;
- signed terms, confidentiality, security, and data-handling terms;
- invoice and payment processor identifiers;
- settlement evidence;
- delivery receipt and customer acceptance;
- refund, warranty, support, and renewal obligations.

## Public reporting rule

Public documentation may say that a fixed-price pilot offer exists. It must not identify a customer without permission or claim bookings, revenue, users, pilots, contracts, or acceptance until the matching private evidence exists.

## Weekly review

For each open commercial item, record the current ladder stage, evidence link, next owner action, due date, and blocking dependency. Keep forecast value, contracted value, invoiced value, collected cash, and recognized revenue in separate fields.
