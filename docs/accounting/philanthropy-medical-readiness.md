# SKYGRID philanthropy and medical-foundation readiness

This module prepares SKYGRID-protocol for a larger philanthropic program as
evidence-backed income begins to settle.

## Existing commitment

The current `hrj=2` accounting rule designates 100% of positive,
evidence-backed net realized income for philanthropic debt relief. This change
does **not** silently rewrite that commitment.

## Medical-foundation track

A separate medical-foundation reserve is now modeled, but its percentage remains
unset until the owner explicitly amends the allocation. This prevents the same
dollar from being counted simultaneously as 100% debt relief and as a medical
reserve.

The working label `SKYGRID Medical Foundation` is operational planning language
only. It is not a representation that a nonprofit corporation, private
foundation, public charity, tax-exempt entity, bank account, board, or legal
trust currently exists.

Before external launch, complete:
- jurisdiction selection;
- entity and governance formation;
- conflict-of-interest and grantmaking controls;
- tax/legal review;
- separate banking/accounting;
- beneficiary/grantee diligence;
- board or authorized-governance approval.

## Revenue gate

Only positive `net_realized_income_usd` may enter the allocation calculation.
Projected, contracted, accrued, unrealized, or unverified economics remain
ineligible.

## Execution boundary

This module is designation-only. It cannot send money, sign wallets, broadcast
transactions, select beneficiaries, or make grants automatically.
